📜 Daily Poem

Still I Rise

Maya Angelou · 🔗 Academy of American Poets

The speaker addresses those who have tried to diminish her through lies, cruelty, and violence, answering each attempt with the same unshakeable refrain: she will rise. Angelou builds the poem through recurring natural images — dust, tides, suns — to suggest that her resilience is as inevitable as the movements of the physical world. By the close, the “I” widens into a collective voice carrying the hope and endurance of an entire lineage into a clear new day.

Why today? A fitting choice for Labor Day — a poem about refusing to be worked down or diminished, and rising anyway.

🎨 Art of the Day

Roberto Matta
“I think that the role of the painter is to search not for the other reality but for the real reality. It is with the mental eye, creating a poetic gesture, how the truth can be reconstructed.”
— attributed

Gustave Courbet · The Calm Sea (1869)

🔗 View at The Met · Oil on canvas · Public Domain

Courbet painted this view of the English Channel during a visit to Étretat on the Normandy coast in August 1869. The tide has receded, leaving two small boats stranded on the sand beneath an immense, quiet sky — an unusually still composition for a marine painter better known for crashing waves.

Roberto Matta · Untitled (1949)

🔗 View at The Met · Oil on canvas · © Artists Rights Society (ARS), New York

Two upright, hybrid figures confront each other across a tense field of gestural marks and hot color. The painting exemplifies the “automatic” techniques Matta absorbed from André Breton’s Surrealists, and his biomorphic abstraction would go on to influence the young Jackson Pollock and the birth of Abstract Expressionism in 1940s New York.

🔗 NBC News

Tyson Foods layoffs leave 2,500 workers without jobs overnight

Tyson abruptly shut its beef processing plant in Joslin, Illinois, on August 14, laying off roughly 2,500 mostly immigrant workers with no advance warning — closure paperwork was handed out on the spot. The company points to a 75-year low in the US cattle herd, driven partly by drought and rising diesel costs; laid-off workers are pushing Illinois for six months of full severance instead of the one-to-four weeks offered. On this Labor Day, the closure has become a flashpoint over how abruptly American workers can lose their livelihoods.

🌍 International

Germany’s far-right AfD wins historic Saxony-Anhalt election, falls just short of a majority

The Alternative for Germany party took 43.8% of the vote in Sunday’s state election — more than double its 2021 result and over twice the tally of Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s Christian Democrats (17.2%, their worst-ever showing there). AfD won 39 of 83 seats, three short of an outright majority, and it remains unclear whether it can form the first far-right state government in Germany since World War II. Turnout hit a high 77.8%.
